A newspaper front page that sums up Friday 20 November 2026 in a single
image — 35 stories: Russia fires 400+ drones and 110 missiles at Ukraine
overnight, two Russian missiles land in Poland and NATO calls it a grave
violation, the Nasdaq plunges 5.28% as Nvidia sinks on a report the SEC is
examining its accounting, GTA VI banks $3.79bn on day one, Trump threatens
a "tremendous" tax on US firms building AI data centres abroad, COP31 talks
run into overtime, Iran says two tankers hit mines in the Strait of Hormuz,
the UK and EU strike trade and travel deals, Saudi Arabia intercepts more
Houthi missiles, a judge approves the $7.25bn Bayer Roundup settlement,
Harden posts his 50th 50-point game, and Estonia closes its border with
Russia — plus 23 numbered one-line briefs, a "WHERE IT HAPPENED" world map,
a big-numbers stat strip, and a DATE CHECK box naming the big stories left
out because they happened on another day.

Every headline is a verified 20 November 2026 event (sources in
`../dev/STORY-POOL.md` of the build workspace); one-party claims stay
attributed. The image is drawn deterministically by `gen.py` (Pillow, fixed
seed, no network, no timestamps) and written to `entry.png` (1024×1024).
`main.ts` copies `entry.png` into `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`.
